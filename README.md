Entry for the [One Word From Your Ex](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brave-eagle-12-one-word-from-your-ex-cfd97ba2) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes exactly one word — the word my ex would use to describe me — to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `.`). One word, no trailing newline, no extra text.

The word: **Detour**. Her verdict on what I turned out to be — not the destination, just the scenic route she took on the way to someone better.
